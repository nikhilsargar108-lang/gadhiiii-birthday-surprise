Happy birthday, my love. ❤️

Sometimes I still think about how strange and beautiful it is that we met on Snapchat. Out of all the conversations and all the people in the world, somehow I found you.

I love your eyes. I love your hair. But more than all of that, I love the kind, caring and loving person you are. Those are the things that make you you, and those are the things I never want to take for granted.

I still remember our first long call — 3 hours and 36 minutes. It was just a call, but it became one of my favorite memories because it was one of the first times I realized how easy it was to lose track of time with you.

I wish I could be beside you today, annoy you in person, make you laugh, give you a proper birthday hug and celebrate with you. Until I can do that, I hope this little surprise reminds you that even when we're far apart, you are still very close to my heart.

So today, I just want you to know: I'm grateful for you, I'm proud to have you in my life, and I'm looking forward to all the memories we haven't made yet.

Happy birthday, Gadhiiiiiiiiiiiii. ❤️
